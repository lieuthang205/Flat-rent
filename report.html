Data

  • rents-input-flats-city-of-sydney.csv (65,777 flats)
  • rents-past-leases-city-of-sydney.csv, filtered to adverts dated ≤ 31 July 2026, $150-$5000/wk, de-duplicated
  • blocks-homes-and-people-2021-2026.csv (block census counts and 2026 estimates)

Method

  1. Monthly bedroom-adjusted median log rent index; Holt / Holt-Winters smoothing weights tuned by L-BFGS-B; projected to Aug-Sep 2026.
  2. Gradient boosting with absolute-error loss (matches the median-error ranking) on de-trended log rent.
  3. Features: latitude, longitude, dist_cbd_km, bedrooms_f, bathrooms_f, car_flag, car_spaces_f, floor_level, flats_in_building, block_new_homes_2021_2026, block_homes_2026, block_growth, ever_advertised_for_lease, first_listed_year, attrs_year, suburb_code, ptype_code, blk_homes_2021, blk_homes_2026, blk_people_2021, blk_people_2026, blk_new_homes, own_prev_lp, own_prev_n, bld_bed_prev_lp, bld_bed_prev_n, bld_prev_lp, bld_prev_n.
  4. History features use only strictly earlier adverts during training (no leakage).
  5. Each flat predicted twice (car_space = 1 / 0) with a monotonic constraint, so the difference is the parking premium. Rounded to $0.50.
